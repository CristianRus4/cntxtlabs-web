SafePlate

SafePlate replaces the paper diary in a New Zealand kitchen. Fridge temperatures, deliveries, cooking and cooling checks, cleaning, training and sickness records are made when the work happens, kept for four years, and ready for a verifier visit.

It runs on the web, Android and iPhone. One subscription covers the whole team. Records save on the phone first if the kitchen is offline, then upload when the signal returns.
