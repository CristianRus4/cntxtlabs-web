SafePlate

Food safety records, kept as the work happens.

Websitesafeplate.nz
Contactinfo@cntxtlabs.co

Food businesses in New Zealand have to keep records: fridge temperatures, deliveries, cooking and cooling, cleaning, training and staff sickness. In most kitchens that still means a paper diary, often filled in late and hard to find when the verifier arrives.

SafePlate replaces the diary. The team records each check on a phone as they do it, and everything is kept for four years, ready to show. It works offline and catches up when the signal returns, and one subscription covers the whole team on the web, Android and iPhone.
