Get in touch

The best way to reach us is by email. It is the only address we use, and it is read every day by the people who build the apps, not by a support desk.

If something isn't working in one of the apps, tell us which app it is, which device and version of iOS you are using, and what happened. That is usually enough to sort it out in one reply. Ideas are just as welcome, and a good number of the features in the apps today started as an email.

Press, review copies, promo codes and partnerships go to the same address. We answer journalists and reviewers first.
