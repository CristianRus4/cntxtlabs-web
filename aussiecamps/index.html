AussieCamps

Campsites, caravan parks, national park sites, rest areas and dump points right across Australia, in one map that keeps working when the signal stops. Real time alerts, no subscription.

Free camper, vanlifer, backpacker or a family three days into a road trip, it is the same idea: plan it faster, get further out, camp somewhere better.

It is the Australian version of KiwiCamping, rebuilt around Australian data.

It is not on the App Store yet. When it lands, it will be here.

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account to create and no personal data gets collected or stored.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which keeps only what is needed to check a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

Google services are used for crash reports, basic anonymous usage stats and some of the features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or handed on to anyone.

Anything else, send an email.
