AussieCamps

AussieCamps helps you find a place to stay in Australia and plan the road trip around it. It has more than 74,000 places, from free camps, national park campgrounds and caravan parks to rest areas, dump points, drinking water and toilets, all stored on your phone, so the details are there in the outback, the bush or on a quiet stretch of coast.

Filter for what you need, like free camping with toilets and water around the Gold Coast, and open any place to check fees, rules, access notes, photos, alerts, the weather and Street View. Save places into your own collections, and they sort themselves as you mark them liked, starred or visited.

Then plan the trip. Put your stops in the order you will drive, see the distance and time for every leg and the whole journey, add dates, notes and a forecast for each stop, and tick them off as you go. Keep a checklist and a packing list beside it, share the itinerary and sync it to your calendar.

It also includes 21 Australian road trips and more than 50 guides on free camps, national park campgrounds, realistic driving days and costs. It works the same way as KiwiCamping, with a directory built for Australia, for campervans, caravans, 4WDs, cars and tents.

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account, and we don't collect or store personal data.

Purchases go through RevenueCat, which keeps only what it needs to confirm a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

We use Google services for crash reports, anonymous usage statistics and a few features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.
