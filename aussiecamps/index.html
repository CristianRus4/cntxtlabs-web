AussieCamps

Every campsite in Australia, in your pocket.

Apps Contact

AussieCamps maps campsites, caravan parks, national park sites, rest areas and dump points right across Australia. Offline-ready, with real-time alerts, beautifully designed, and no subscriptions.

Whether you are a free camper, a vanlifer, a backpacker or a family on a road trip, AussieCamps helps you plan smarter, roam further and camp better.

It is the same app as our New Zealand edition, KiwiCamping, rebuilt around Australian data.
