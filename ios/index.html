iOS apps

A few apps on the App Store, for health, everyday habits and making time. They are small, focused tools rather than a single suite, each built around one job and meant to stay out of the way. All of them run on iPhone, and none of them require an account to start using.

  • ftnss A private AI coach over Apple Health, for training, nutrition, sleep and recovery.
  • SecretApp This is an app built in stealth, with no plans to be revealed for now. Sorry!
  • Bites Calories and macros from a description of the meal, using Apple Intelligence.
  • NoSnus A quit plan for nicotine pouches, built around your habit, with a date on the calendar.
  • NotSoBusy A picture of when you are busy, from your calendars, with none of the event details.
