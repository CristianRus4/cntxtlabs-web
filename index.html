About Contact

cntxt is a software company based in New Zealand. It builds applications for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, together with the websites that support them.

For over a decade the work has focused on how design and technology influence the way people behave, and on turning that into software people continue to use. The apps cover time and attention, health and fitness, and the outdoors.

They have been downloaded by more than two million people around the world.

Apps

  • Left→ The time you have left, made visible, with habits, countdowns and a daily planner.
  • txtpod→ Articles, links and PDFs converted into a private podcast feed.
  • ftnss→ Training, nutrition and recovery in a single coach built on Apple Health.
  • KiwiCamping→ Campsites, huts and holiday parks across New Zealand, available offline.
  • AussieCamps→ Campsites, caravan parks and rest areas across Australia. In development.
  • NoSnus→ A tapering plan for nicotine pouches, built from your own consumption.
  • Bites→ Calories, macros and nutrients calculated from a description of your meal.
  • NotSoBusy→ Availability shared without giving access to your calendar.
