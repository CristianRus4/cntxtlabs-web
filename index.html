Cntxt Labs (pronounced “context”) is a small software company founded by Cristian Rus. We design and build apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, for the web and for Android.

For more than ten years our work has been about how design changes what people do. We pick an everyday problem, build the simplest thing that solves it, and keep improving it for as long as people rely on it.

Today, 1,760,542 people around the world use what we make. Revenue is up 22% this month, and the codebase has 1,737 contributions so far.

If you have a question about one of the apps, or something you would like to build together, get in touch.

  • Left Time is easy to ignore until it runs out. Left keeps it in view, next to your habits and plans. Used by more than 1.5 million people.
  • SafePlate Food safety records for New Zealand kitchens, kept on a phone as the work happens instead of in a paper diary.
  • KiwiCamping & AussieCamps Camping maps and road-trip planners, one for New Zealand and one for Australia, with thousands of places on your phone and a planner for every stop.
  • txtpod For everything you save and never get to read. txtpod reads it out in full, in a natural voice, as episodes in your podcast app.
  • NoSnus A step-down plan off nicotine pouches that limits both pouches and milligrams, with a pouch-free date to aim for.
