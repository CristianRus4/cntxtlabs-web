Cntxt Labs (pronounced “context”) is a design-focused company founded by Cristian Rus, specialised in building software products and services across Apple platforms, web, and Android.

For over a decade the work has focused on how design and technology influence the way people behave, and on turning that into software people continue to use. The product themes cover time and attention, health and fitness, and the outdoors.

Today, 1,760,542 people use these products around the world. Revenue is showing 22% growth this month. The codebase includes 1,737 contributions.

For enquiries about the products or working together, get in contact.

  • Left An iOS app that makes the time you have left visible, with habits, streaks, countdowns and a planner for the day, used by over 1.5 million people around the world.
  • Camping apps Offline maps for campsites, holiday parks and the road, with a trip planner, for New Zealand and Australia.
  • SafePlate Food safety records for New Zealand kitchens, kept as the work happens, on web, Android and iPhone.
  • txtpod A service that converts text to audio with HiFi AI voices and sends it to your podcast, available as an iOS app and a web app.
  • Other iOS apps A few more apps on the App Store, for health, everyday habits and making time.
