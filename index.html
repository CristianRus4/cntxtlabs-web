cntxt is a studio based in Wellington, New Zealand. We design and build apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and the websites that go with them.

We are interested in how design and technology shape behaviour, and in the space where form and function meet. The aim is software that is genuinely useful, careful with your attention, and quietly well made.

We have spent over ten years shipping software on Apple platforms. Our apps are used by more than two million people around the world.

Almost everything we build runs on your device first. No accounts where accounts are not needed, no tracking, no ads.
