cntxt is a software company based in New Zealand. It builds apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and the websites that go with them.

For over a decade the work here has come back to the same question: how design and technology shape the way people behave. The apps that come out of it are small, opinionated, and meant to be lived with rather than admired. Some are about time, some about health, some about getting outside.

They have been downloaded by more than two million people around the world.

Apps

  • Left. Time you can see, so you actually use it. Habits, streaks, countdowns and a planner for the day.
  • txtpod. Turns articles, links and PDFs into a private podcast feed you can play anywhere.
  • ftnss. Training, food and recovery pulled together into one coach that reads your Apple Health data.
  • KiwiCamping. Every campsite, hut and holiday park in New Zealand, working offline.
  • AussieCamps. The same thing for Australia. Not out yet.
  • NoSnus. Quit nicotine pouches on a plan built from your own logs instead of a generic one.
  • Bites. Calories, macros and nutrients worked out from a sentence about what you ate.
  • NotSoBusy. Show people when you are free without handing over your whole calendar.
