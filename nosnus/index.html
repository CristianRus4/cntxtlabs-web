NoSnus

NoSnus builds a plan for stopping nicotine pouches based on your own consumption. Rather than applying a fixed schedule, it uses your logs to set a tapering rate that matches your habits and can be sustained.

The approach follows established methods for reducing nicotine dependence: lower the dose gradually, and keep cravings within a manageable range. Every pouch is logged, and progress is tracked until you have stopped.

Download NoSnus from the App Store.

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account to create, and no personal data is collected or stored.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which retains only what is required to verify a purchase and restore it if the app is reinstalled.

Google services are used for crash reporting, anonymous usage statistics and certain features within the app. None of this identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.

For anything else, send an email.
