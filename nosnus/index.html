NoSnus

NoSnus is for getting off nicotine pouches, on a plan that comes from your own numbers. Instead of one schedule handed to everybody, it reads what you actually use and how you log it, then works out a taper that fits your habits and moves at a pace you can hold.

The method behind it is the one the research supports: bring the dose down gradually, keep the cravings inside what you can handle. Log every pouch, watch the count fall, and keep going until there is nothing left to quit.

You can get NoSnus on the App Store.

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account to create and no personal data gets collected or stored.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which keeps only what is needed to check a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

Google services are used for crash reports, basic anonymous usage stats and some of the features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or handed on to anyone.

Anything else, send an email.
