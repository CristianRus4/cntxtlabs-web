NoSnus helps you quit nicotine pouches with a plan built for you. Instead of a one-size-fits-all approach, it looks at your real consumption and your daily logs to design a tapering path that fits your habits and moves at a pace you can actually stick to.

Every plan is grounded in methods shown to work for reducing nicotine dependence, lowering your intake gradually while keeping cravings manageable. Log each pouch, track your progress, and watch your dependence shrink day by day until you are finally free.
