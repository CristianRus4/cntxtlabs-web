NoSnus

NoSnus is built for one thing: getting off nicotine pouches. Zyn, Velo and snus, not cigarettes or vapes. You answer a few questions about how many you use, how strong they are and how soon after waking you reach for the first one, and NoSnus builds a step-down plan with a projected pouch-free date.

Each phase of the plan sets two limits, how many pouches a day and how many milligrams, because fewer pouches at a higher strength is the same nicotine. After the first week the plan recalibrates to what you actually logged, and if a phase is too hard the target moves instead of you failing it. A bad day costs that day, not the week.

Logging takes one tap from a Home Screen widget. Tag what set off a slip, like stress, boredom, driving or a meal, and over a few weeks you see which moments cost you most. When a craving peaks, Breathe walks you through it, and the cravings you get past are counted too, along with the money you are no longer spending.

There is no account, no email and no feed. NoSnus supports a behavioural plan and is not a medical device. If you want clinical support to stop using nicotine, talk to a doctor, a pharmacist or your national quitline.
