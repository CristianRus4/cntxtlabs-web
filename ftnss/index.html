Workouts, nutrition and recovery in one AI coach.

Apps Contact

ftnss connects your training, your food and your recovery in a single conversation. It builds adaptive plans across strength, endurance, mobility and recovery, and adjusts them as your week actually goes.

Nutrition is goal-based, with macro targets and logging by photo or text. It syncs deeply with Apple Health — HRV, VO2 Max, sleep — logs your sessions automatically, and reads training load to tell you when to push and when to back off.

Progress shows up in visual dashboards for weight, energy and performance, alongside more than fifteen in-app cards covering workouts, steps, sleep and longer-term trends.

Privacy policy

cntxt (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the ftnss app (the “Service”).

Your health data stays on your device. Syncing happens through Apple Health, you choose what ftnss is allowed to read, and you can change those permissions at any time in Apple Health settings. We do not collect or store your health data ourselves.

To provide coaching and insights, some anonymised data is sent to an AI provider for processing. It is stripped of identifiers and used solely to generate your suggestions.

Purchase-related information is managed securely by RevenueCat and used only to verify and restore purchases if you reinstall the app.

The full policy is available at ftnss.app/privacy. Questions? Email info@cntxtlabs.co anytime.
