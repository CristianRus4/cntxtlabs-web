ftnss

ftnss brings training, nutrition and recovery into a single coach. It builds plans across strength, endurance, mobility and recovery, then adjusts them according to how the week actually progresses.

Nutrition is tracked against your goals, with macro targets and logging by photo or text. The app reads Apple Health in full, including HRV, VO2 Max and sleep, records sessions automatically, and uses training load to indicate when to push and when to recover.

Progress is presented through dashboards for weight, energy and performance, alongside more than fifteen cards covering workouts, steps, sleep and longer term trends.

Privacy

Your health data remains on your device. It is synchronised through Apple Health, you choose what ftnss is permitted to read, and that permission can be changed at any time in the Health app. None of it is collected or stored by cntxt.

To produce plans and insights, some anonymised data is sent for processing by an AI model. Identifiers are removed, and the data is used only to generate what you requested. The full policy can be read at ftnss.app/privacy.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which retains only what is required to verify a purchase and restore it if the app is reinstalled.

Google services are used for crash reporting, anonymous usage statistics and certain features within the app. None of this identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.

For anything else, send an email.
