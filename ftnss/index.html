ftnss

ftnss puts your training, your food and your recovery in the same conversation. It builds plans across strength, endurance, mobility and recovery, then changes them as the week actually goes rather than as it was supposed to.

Food is tracked against goals, with macro targets and logging by photo or by typing a sentence. It reads Apple Health properly, HRV and VO2 Max and sleep included, logs sessions on its own, and uses training load to say when to push and when to back off.

Progress shows up in dashboards for weight, energy and performance, alongside more than fifteen cards covering workouts, steps, sleep and longer trends.

You can get ftnss on the App Store, or read more about it first.

Privacy

Your health data stays on your device. It syncs through Apple Health, you pick what ftnss is allowed to read, and you can change that at any time in the Health app. None of it gets collected or stored by cntxt.

To write your plans and insights, some anonymised data is sent off to be processed by an AI model. It has your identifiers stripped out and is only used to generate what you asked for. The full detail is in the ftnss privacy policy.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which keeps only what is needed to check a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

Google services are used for crash reports, basic anonymous usage stats and some of the features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or handed on to anyone.

Anything else, send an email.
