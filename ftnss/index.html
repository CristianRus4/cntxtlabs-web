ftnss

Your health data, turned into advice.

DownloadScan with your iPhone
Website

Your watch, your running app and your food log each know part of the story, but none of them tells you what to do next. ftnss reads it all through Apple Health and turns it into advice you can act on.

It looks at your training, sleep, nutrition and recovery, including data from Apple Watch, Strava, Garmin and MyFitnessPal. It builds plans for strength, endurance, mobility and recovery, adjusts them as your week changes, and answers questions about any of it. You can log a meal from a photo or a sentence.

Progress shows up in dashboards for weight, energy and performance, along with your workouts, steps, sleep and longer-term trends.
