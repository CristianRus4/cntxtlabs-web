Bites privacy policy

This policy covers Bites on iPhone and iPad, made by Cntxt Labs in New Zealand.

The app runs on your device. There is no account, and we don't collect or store personal data.

Purchases go through RevenueCat, which keeps only what it needs to confirm a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

We use Google services for crash reports, anonymous usage statistics and a few features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.

Questions about privacy? Send us an email.
