Bites

Log a meal in one sentence.

DownloadiPhone and iPad

Most people stop counting calories because logging food takes too long. Bites makes it one sentence: describe what you ate, and Apple Intelligence works out the calories and macros.

It syncs with Apple Health, tracks your daily goal, and shows how much energy you have left once your activity is counted. Meals you eat often can be saved and logged again with a tap.

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account, and we don't collect or store personal data.

Purchases go through RevenueCat, which keeps only what it needs to confirm a purchase and restore it if you reinstall the app.

We use Google services for crash reports, anonymous usage statistics and a few features inside the app. None of it identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.

Questions about privacy? Send us an email.
