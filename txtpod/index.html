txtpod

txtpod takes anything worth reading and gives it back as a podcast episode. Save an article, a link or a PDF once, then listen to it wherever you already listen to things.

Over thirty natural voices across more than twenty languages, with translation if you want it. Everything lands in a private feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts or anything else that speaks RSS.

It will also write episodes on topics you pick and a daily brief on the subjects you follow, with its own artwork, listening streaks, a web app that stays in sync, and hooks into Instapaper, Matter and Readwise.

You can get txtpod on the App Store, or read more about it first.
