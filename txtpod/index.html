txtpod

The articles you save, read aloud.

DownloadiPhone and iPad
Websitetxtpod.app

Most of us save more to read than we ever get through. txtpod turns those articles into podcast episodes, so you can listen to them while you walk, drive or cook.

Send it an article, a link or a PDF, and a natural voice reads it into a private feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Overcast or any other podcast app. There are more than thirty voices across more than twenty languages, and it can translate as it reads.

It can also make episodes about topics you follow and a short daily brief, and it connects to Instapaper, Matter and Readwise. It works on iPhone, iPad and the web.
