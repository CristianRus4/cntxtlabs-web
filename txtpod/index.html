txtpod

Text to speech, delivered as a podcast.

Apps Contact

txtpod turns anything worth reading into a podcast episode. Save an article, a link or a PDF once, and listen to it anywhere.

Episodes are narrated by more than thirty natural voices across over twenty languages, with automatic translation when you want it. Everything lands in a private RSS feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts and anything else that speaks RSS.

It also generates episodes on topics you choose and daily news briefs on the subjects you follow, with custom artwork, listening streaks, a web app that syncs, and integrations with Instapaper, Matter and Readwise.
