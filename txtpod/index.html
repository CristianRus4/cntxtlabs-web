txtpod

txtpod converts articles, links and PDFs into narrated podcast episodes. Save something once, and listen to it wherever you already listen.

Episodes are read by more than thirty natural voices across over twenty languages, with translation available. They are delivered to a private feed that works in Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts and any other player that supports RSS.

txtpod also generates episodes on topics you choose and a daily brief on the subjects you follow, with custom artwork, listening streaks, a synchronised web app, and integrations with Instapaper, Matter and Readwise.
