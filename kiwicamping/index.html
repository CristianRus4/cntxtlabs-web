KiwiCamping

Camping in New Zealand often means finding a site after the signal has gone, and knowing the rules once you get there. KiwiCamping keeps more than 6,500 places on your phone, from DOC campsites and huts to freedom camping spots, holiday parks and dump stations, so they open anywhere.

Filter by what you need, save the places you like, and turn them into a trip with routes, distances, dates and notes. Short guides explain how DOC sites, huts and freedom camping work, so you know what to expect before you arrive.
