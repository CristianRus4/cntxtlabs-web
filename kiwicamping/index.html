KiwiCamping

KiwiCamping helps you find a place to camp in New Zealand and plan the road trip around it. It has more than 6,500 places, from DOC campsites, huts and freedom camping areas to holiday parks, dump stations and useful stops, and every one is stored on your phone, so it still opens where the signal does not reach.

Filter for what matters on the night, like free camping with toilets and water near Queenstown, and open any place to see photos, fees, facilities, current alerts, the weather and Street View of the turnoff. Save places into collections for a region or a shortlist, and they sort themselves as you mark them liked, starred or visited.

The trip planner is where it comes together. Order your stops the way you will drive them, see the distance and driving time for every leg, add dates, notes and the forecast for each stop, and tick them off as you travel. Keep a packing list beside it and sync the whole trip to your calendar.

KiwiCamping also includes 21 road-trip routes, from Northland to the East Cape, and more than 50 practical guides on how DOC sites, huts and freedom camping work, planning realistic driving days and comparing costs.
