How it works

  1. Watches

    Disk, memory, CPU and GPU, against limits you set.

  2. Your agent looks first

    Before the alert, it reads what’s filling your Mac. Read-only, in a sandbox.

  3. You tick

    What’s safe to clean or quit, with sizes and reasons.

  4. Levo cleans

    Only what you ticked. Anything that can’t be rebuilt goes to the Trash.
