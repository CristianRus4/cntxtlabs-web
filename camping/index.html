KiwiCamping & AussieCamps

Find the camp, then plan the whole road trip.

KiwiCampingScan with your iPhone
AussieCampsScan with your iPhone

KiwiCamping and AussieCamps help you decide where to stay tonight and where to go next. Each is a camping map and a road-trip planner in one, built the same way: KiwiCamping for New Zealand and AussieCamps for Australia.

Start with the map. KiwiCamping has more than 6,500 places across New Zealand and AussieCamps more than 74,000 across Australia: campsites, holiday and caravan parks, national park sites and free camping areas, along with rest areas, dump stations, drinking water and toilets. Filter for exactly what you need, like free camping with toilets and water nearby, and every place opens with photos, fees, facilities, alerts, the weather and Street View of the entrance.

Then plan the trip. Add the places you like as stops, put them in the order you will drive, and see the distance and driving time for every leg and for the whole journey. Each stop has its own date, notes and forecast, and you tick it off as you go. Keep a packing list beside the itinerary, share it, and sync it to your calendar.

Both apps come with detailed road-trip routes and practical guides, from how DOC sites and freedom camping work in New Zealand to free camps and national parks in Australia. And because the whole directory is stored on your phone, the details are still there after the last bar of signal.
