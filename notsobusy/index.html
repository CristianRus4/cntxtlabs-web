NotSoBusy

NotSoBusy shares your availability for a day or a week without sharing your calendar. Recipients see which periods are taken, and nothing about what is in them.

There is no account and no login, and no data leaves your device.

Download

Privacy

The app runs on your device. There is no account to create, and no personal data is collected or stored.

Purchases are handled by RevenueCat, which retains only what is required to verify a purchase and restore it if the app is reinstalled.

Google services are used for crash reporting, anonymous usage statistics and certain features within the app. None of this identifies you, and none of it is sold or shared.

For anything else, send an email.
