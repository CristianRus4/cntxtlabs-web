Left shows the time you have left. The year, the month, the week, the day, the hour, the minute and your life, drawn as symbols that dim as they pass.

Habits can run daily, several times a day, weekly, or to a schedule you set. Streaks are there to protect, aims count anything you can build up or cut down, and a habit or a streak can be shared with a friend. Dates that matter sit ahead of you as countdowns, each with its own list of things to get done, imported from the calendar if you want them to be.

The planner puts that next to the rest of the day: Apple Calendar, Reminders, the weather, sleep and workouts, in one view. A fasting tracker, a focus timer and a mood log sit in the same place, so the day is planned where it is spent.
