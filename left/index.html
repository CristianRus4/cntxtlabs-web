Left

Left shows the time you have left. The year, the month, the week, the day, the hour, the minute and your life, drawn as symbols that dim as they pass.

Alongside it sits a habit tracker with streaks and goals, countdowns for the dates ahead, and a planner that brings together your calendar, reminders, health data and the weather. It also includes a fasting tracker, a focus timer, a mood tracker, and progress you can share with friends.

There are more than twenty widgets across the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch, along with a dedicated Apple Watch app.

Download Left from the App Store, or read more about it on its own site.
