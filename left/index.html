Left

Keep the time you have left in view.

DownloadiPhone, iPad and Apple Watch
Websitegetleft.app

Most of us only notice time when it is running out. Left keeps it in view: today, this week, this year and a whole life, drawn as symbols that fade as they pass.

Seeing time pass is a good reason to use it well, so Left helps with that too. Build the habits you care about and keep the streaks going, count down to the dates that matter, and share a goal with a friend.

The planner brings your calendar, reminders, weather, sleep and workouts into one place, so you plan the day where you can see it going. More than 1.5 million people use Left on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
