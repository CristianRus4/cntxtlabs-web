Left visualises the time you have left and helps you make the most of it. See the year, month, week, day, hour, minute and your life laid out as symbols that dim as time passes.

It carries a habit tracker with streaks and goals, countdowns for the dates that pull you forward, and a planner that brings together your calendar, reminders, health data and the weather. There is a fasting tracker, a focus timer, a mood tracker, and friend sharing when you want someone holding you to it.

More than twenty widgets across the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch, plus a Watch app of its own.
