Left

Left shows you the time you have left. The year, the month, the week, the day, the hour, the minute and your life, laid out as symbols that dim as they go.

Around that sits a habit tracker with streaks and goals, countdowns for the dates worth waiting for, and a planner that pulls in your calendar, reminders, health data and the weather. There is a fasting tracker, a focus timer, a mood tracker, and a way to share progress with a friend when you need someone keeping you honest.

Over twenty widgets across the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch, plus a Watch app of its own.

You can get Left on the App Store, or read more about it first.
